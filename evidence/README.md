# Evidence

## Purpose

The `evidence` platform validates organizational information before it becomes part of the Organizational Brain's reasoning process.

## Why It Exists

Reliable intelligence requires trustworthy evidence. This platform ensures that observations are verified, assessed for quality, and supported before they influence organizational understanding or executive decision-making.

## Future Scope

This platform will contain evidence validation, confidence assessment, source verification, data quality evaluation, traceability, and supporting mechanisms for evidence-based intelligence.
