# OBA Core MVP — Scope Boundary

**Status:** Proposed, awaiting owner sign-off (decisions S-1…S-6 at the bottom)
**Date:** 2026-09-29
**Replaces:** the current 17-page sidebar as the definition of "the MVP"

---

## 1. The problem, in one paragraph

The product answers one question well and fifteen questions badly. Today there are
17 pages, 136 components and ~58 API mounts. The same few questions (who is a single
point of failure? what's our score? what if X leaves?) are answered 4–7 times each,
with different rules and different labels, and several pages compute their own
answers in the browser. An executive sees "79 PARTIAL" on one card, "67 WARNING"
on another, "Culture Health", "DNA Fingerprint", an "Industry Benchmark" against
targets we invented, and a person marked CRITICAL because a database row is
missing. None of it can be traced to a fact they recognise. That is why it looks
like it has no direction: it doesn't.

## 2. What we sell (the direction)

**Horquva finds the places where your organisation is one person, one AI agent or
one vendor away from something breaking — and tells you what to fix first.**

That is the Executive Dependency Scan (Blueprint §1.3). Everything in the MVP exists
to produce, explain, or act on that scan. If a page doesn't, it's out.

The buyer's four questions, and the only four the MVP answers:

| # | Buyer question | MVP answer |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | **Where are we exposed?** | Critical assets with no owner, no backup, or no documentation — and which of those facts we simply don't know yet |
| Q2 | **Who / what is a single point of failure?** | People, agents, tools and vendors whose loss breaks critical things, with the list of what breaks |
| Q3 | **What happens if X leaves or fails?** | Pick a person, agent, tool or vendor → named list of what stops working and who's left holding it; test a successor |
| Q4 | **What do we do first?** | One ranked action list; each action names the fact it fixes |

Plus a chat Agent that answers these same four questions in plain language, from the same data.

## 3. The data model the whole product stands on

One classification, computed once in the backend (`domain/definitions.js`), read by every page and the agent.

**Things we track (assets):** AI agents · workflows · AI tools/platforms · vendors/models.
**People:** employees, only in their role as owners/backups of assets.

**Every asset carries exactly these facts:**

| Fact | Values | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Criticality | critical / high / medium / low / **not recorded** | recorded by customer |
| Owner | named person / **none** / **not recorded** | owners row |
| Backup | named person / **none** / **not recorded** | owners row |
| Documented | yes / **no** / **not recorded** | knowledge_assets row |
| Depends on | list of assets | dependencies |

**The rule: "not recorded" is never "no".** A missing row is a gap in *our knowledge*,
shown as a question to answer, never as a risk verdict. Only a row that actually says
"no backup" produces "no backup".

**Derived, and only these:**

- **Exposed asset** = critical/high AND (owner = none OR backup = none OR documented = no).
- **Single point of failure** = an asset or person whose loss leaves at least one critical
  asset with no one able to run it. Always shown *with* the list of what breaks.
- **Blast radius** = the named list (and count) of assets downstream of X. Shown as
  names, not as a 0–100 number.
- **Concentration** = "Person/vendor/model X underpins N of M critical assets." A share, in words.
- **Unknowns list** = every "not recorded" fact on a critical asset. This is the scan's
  intake questionnaire — the customer fills it in, and the scan gets sharper.

## 4. The rules every screen must follow

1. **Backend computes, frontend displays.** No scoring, thresholds, verdicts, or
   averaging in `frontend/`. `lib/riskIntelligence.ts`, `lib/knowledgeRisk.ts`,
   `lib/aiToolIntelligence.ts`, `lib/graph.ts` (`getDownstream`/`getUpstream`),
   and component-level math (`FivePillarsRadar`, `DependencyPipeline`,
   `MemoryCarriersPanel`, `ContinuityTab`) go away.
2. **One definition per concept.** SPOF, exposed, critical, backup, documented — defined
   once in `definitions.js`. A second implementation is a bug.
3. **"Not recorded" is a third state everywhere** (§3).
4. **Every number is a count of named things you can click through to.**
   "6 critical agents have no backup" → click → the 6 agents → click → the owners row.
   No number without its rows.
5. **No 0–100 indexes on screen in the MVP.** Not health, not pillars, not risk score,
   not "confidence". Engines may still use scores internally for *ordering*; the UI
   shows the order and the reasons, not the number. (Rationale: our CPT weights and pillar
   formulas are authored, not calibrated — see the 2026-09-29 engine review. A count
   of named facts can't be argued with; a "79" can.)
6. **No invented comparisons.** No "industry benchmark", no culture/DNA/maturity, no
   "AI confidence %". If we can't name the rows behind it, it doesn't ship.
7. **Plain English labels.** No module codes (M18, M49, GI/MI/DI/OI), no "Twin",
   "Brain Index", "Universe".
8. **People are shown as roles in a dependency, never ranked or scored.** "Omar holds 3
   critical assets; 2 have no recorded backup" — yes. "Omar: CRITICAL 94" — no. This
   is also required by our US compliance positioning (decision support at role/team
   level, never ranking individuals — see the 2026-09-28 compliance plan).

## 5. The MVP product: 7 pages

| Page | Answers | Built from (today's pieces) |
|---|---|---|
| **Overview** | Q1–Q4 at a glance | Headline (§6), top 5 SPOFs, top 5 actions, unknowns count. Replaces Dashboard. |
| **Assets** | Q1 | One table: every agent, workflow, tool, vendor with criticality / owner / backup / documented / depends-on / blast radius. Filter by type. Row drawer shows evidence rows. Absorbs Risk Intelligence, AI Tool Intelligence, Knowledge Risk's undocumented table, Workflows list. |
| **People** | Q2 (human side) | Key-person dependencies: who holds critical assets, which have no backup / not recorded. Absorbs Ownership, Knowledge Risk (people), Org Memory, Continuity. |
| **Map** | Q2 (structural) | Dependency graph; click a node → its blast radius and what it depends on. Absorbs Dependency Map, Network, hidden-dependency overlay, Org Science graph cards. |
| **What-If** | Q3 | Pick person / agent / tool / vendor → what breaks, who's left holding it; "test a successor" (D-70 reassign). Default view: the ranked list of the most damaging single losses. Absorbs Simulation, Knowledge's DepartureSim, Map's BlastRadiusSimulator, AI Tools' outage panel, Org Science impact card. Other pages link here with the entity preselected. |
| **Actions** | Q4 | One ranked list. Each item: the fact it fixes, the asset, suggested owner. Absorbs Recommendations (all 5 renderings), Decision support queue, dashboard feeds. |
| **Agent** | all | Chat over the same backend definitions. Already exists at `/agent`. |

Plus **Admin → Data** (not a sidebar item in the pitch): the Unknowns list, import, users.

**Sidebar goes from 17 items to 7.**

## 6. The headline

**Recommendation:** replace every headline score with one counted fact:

> **9 of 15 critical assets are fully covered** (owner, backup and documentation recorded).
> 4 exposed · 2 unknown.

Why this instead of `pillars()`: it's the Scan's core finding in one line, every
executive understands it without a legend, and it drills straight down to 15 named
assets. The pillar index (GI/MI/DI/OI) is built from authored formulas the engine
review flagged as needing review, and today two different "headline" numbers (79 and
67) are on screen at once.

This **changes Blueprint Invariant 3** (headline = `pillars()`). The invariant's
*intent* — one headline, computed once, in the backend — is kept. Needs sign-off (S-2).

## 7. What we do NOT do in the MVP (explicit)

**Pages removed from the product:**

| Page | Why it's out |
|---|---|
| Org Science (23 cards) | Culture, DNA, behavioural profile, maturity curve, learning maturity, pattern regularity = invented formulas measuring abstractions. Industry Benchmark compares against our own chosen targets. Useful graph cards move into Map. |
| Forecast | Reads frozen `organizational_forecasts` rows; names carriers the live model says don't exist. |
| Decision Intelligence | Decision history/queues are not part of the dependency scan; two disagreeing queues exist. |
| Continuity & Governance | Third and fourth continuity numbers (M18, M19); "Writes Blocked / 4 Pending Resolves" strip is fabricated. Folded into People. |
| Org Memory | Duplicate SPOF taxonomy (Preserved/Vulnerable/At Risk/Lost). Folded into People. |
| Knowledge Risk | Browser-computed. Split into Assets (undocumented) and People (holders). |
| Network | Same endpoint as Org Science's centrality card. Becomes a Map layer. |
| Workflows (collision detector, self-healing feed, verification ledger) | Not the scan. Workflows themselves become rows in Assets. |

**Components removed:** FivePillarsRadar, Heatmap, VerdictBanner (79/PARTIAL), DailyBriefingCard,
WhatMattersNowFeed (frozen `context_items`), ExecutiveMemoryPanel, EarlyWarningStrip,
VolatilityCard, TwinHealthIndex, TwinSyncStatus, DemoSummary, VerifiedAdvisorPanel,
ReplaceabilityMatrix (authored +40/+30/+30 scale, bench-peer signal saturates),
AutomationStatusStrip, DecisionTrailTable.

**Capabilities explicitly out of scope for the MVP:**

- Any organisational "health", "maturity", "culture" or "intelligence" index shown to users
- Per-person risk scores or rankings of individuals
- Forecasting / trend prediction (no real history exists to back it)
- Volatility / change-over-time views (need weeks of real change events first — post-MVP)
- Replaceability scoring (post-MVP, after the formula is reviewed)
- Industry benchmarks
- Live connectors (Jira/Slack/GitHub). MVP ingest = spreadsheet/CSV + manual edits.
- Multi-org in one instance (one deployment per org — already decided)
- Self-healing, automation, "autonomous" writes by the agent

**Code:** removed pages' backend routes and the M01–M55 brain modules stay in the repo
but are unreachable from the MVP UI and agent. We delete them in a later cleanup, not
during the rebuild — deleting and rebuilding at the same time is how things break silently.

## 8. The 5-minute pitch this scope supports

1. **Overview:** "9 of 15 critical assets are covered. 4 are exposed, 2 we don't know about yet."
2. Click the top SPOF → **What-If** preselected: "If Omar leaves, LogAnalyzer and 2 workflows
   have no one who can run them." Test Yuki as successor → "covered, but Yuki now holds 5 critical assets."
3. **Actions:** "Assign a backup to LogAnalyzer" — the #1 item, tied to that fact.
4. **Agent:** "What breaks if our OpenAI contract lapses?" → answered from the same data, with links.
5. Close: "This took us 48 hours from your spreadsheet. The 2 unknowns are the first thing we'd ask you."

Every claim in that script is a count of named rows. Nothing needs a legend or a caveat.
