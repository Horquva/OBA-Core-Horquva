# Executive

## Purpose

The `executive` platform transforms organizational intelligence into executive decision support by preparing insights, recommendations, and strategic information for leadership.

## Why It Exists

Executives require clear, evidence-based intelligence rather than raw data or technical outputs. This platform bridges the gap between the Organizational Brain and executive decision-making.

## Future Scope

This platform will contain executive intelligence services, strategic recommendations, executive briefings, decision support capabilities, governance interfaces, and other leadership-focused functionality.
