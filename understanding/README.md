# Understanding

## Purpose

The `understanding` platform transforms validated evidence into meaningful organizational insights by interpreting events, relationships, and business context.

## Why It Exists

Evidence alone does not provide intelligence. This platform enables the Organizational Brain to understand what organizational events mean, why they matter, and how they relate to the broader enterprise.

## Future Scope

This platform will contain contextual interpretation, semantic analysis, relationship discovery, organizational context processing, and other capabilities that convert evidence into organizational understanding.
