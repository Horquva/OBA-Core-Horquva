# Constitution

## Purpose

The `constitution` platform contains the foundational engineering principles, architectural rules, governance policies, and constitutional documents that define how the Organizational Brain is designed, implemented, and evolved.

## Why It Exists

This platform serves as the authoritative source for architectural decisions, engineering methodology, standards, and governance. It ensures that every component of the system follows a consistent constitutional framework.

## Future Scope

This platform will contain constitutional documents, engineering guidebooks, architectural principles, governance policies, engineering standards, and other foundational references that guide the development of the Organizational Cognitive Operating System (OCOS).
