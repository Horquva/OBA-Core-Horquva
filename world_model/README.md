# World Model

## Purpose

The `world_model` platform maintains the Living Organizational World Model, representing the current state of the enterprise and the relationships between its people, systems, processes, and knowledge.

## Why It Exists

The Organizational Brain requires a continuously updated representation of organizational reality to perform accurate reasoning, prediction, simulation, and decision support.

## Future Scope

This platform will contain the Living Organizational World Model, organizational state management, relationship mapping, enterprise modeling, and continuous world-state updates.
