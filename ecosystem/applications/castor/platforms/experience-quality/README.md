## About This Platform

The Experience Quality Platform is responsible for establishing quality engineering standards, validation frameworks, testing strategies, review processes, and release readiness practices for the Castor platform.

Quality is treated as a continuous engineering discipline rather than a final testing activity. The platform ensures that every future Horquva product delivers a reliable, consistent, accessible, maintainable, and user-friendly experience.

The primary objective is to define reusable quality standards and governance processes that every engineering platform can adopt throughout the software development lifecycle.
