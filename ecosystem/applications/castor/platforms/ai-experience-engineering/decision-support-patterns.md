# Decision-Support Patterns

## Purpose

This specification defines reusable AI Experience patterns for approved AI-assisted decision support.

The AI Experience layer presents and enables interaction with approved intelligence.

It does not become the source of organizational reasoning.

---

## Decision-Support Flow

Context
↓
AI Interaction
↓
Organizational Insight
↓
Recommendation
↓
Clarification
↓
Decision Support

---

# Context

The experience should present relevant context required for the user to understand the interaction.

Applicable context may include:

- Current workflow context.
- Relevant organizational scope.
- Existing interaction context.
- Available supporting information.

---

# AI Interaction

The user initiates or continues an approved AI interaction.

The interaction may result in:

- Insight
- Recommendation
- Clarification
- Failure
- Follow-Up

---

# Insight Presentation

The AI Experience should maintain a clear distinction between:

- Available context
- Presented insight
- Recommendation
- User action

---

# Recommendation Behavior

When applicable, a recommendation should provide:

- Relevant context.
- The available recommendation.
- Supporting information where available.
- Follow-up actions.
- Clarification access.

---

# Clarification

The user must be able to request or provide clarification when:

- The output requires refinement.
- The recommendation is not sufficiently understood.
- Available context is ambiguous.

---

# Decision Support

The experience supports the user's understanding and interaction with approved intelligence.

The AI Experience layer does not independently:

- Establish organizational reasoning.
- Create organizational authority.
- Replace approved intelligence sources.

---

# User Control

The user must retain control over:

- Requesting clarification.
- Continuing the interaction.
- Refining the request.
- Selecting available next actions.

---

# Executive Workspace Transition

Where applicable:

AI INTERACTION
↓
DECISION-SUPPORT OUTPUT
↓
EXECUTIVE WORKSPACE TRANSITION

The AI Experience layer defines the interaction transition.

The Executive Workspace platform remains responsible for its own platform implementation.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

The Decision-Support Pattern is complete when:

- Context requirements are defined.
- Interaction behavior is defined.
- Insight presentation is defined.
- Recommendation behavior is defined.
- Clarification is supported.
- User control is preserved.
- Executive Workspace transitions are identified.
- Organizational reasoning authority remains outside the AI Experience layer.
