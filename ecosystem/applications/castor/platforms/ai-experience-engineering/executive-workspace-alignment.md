# Executive Workspace Alignment

## Purpose

Define AI Experience requirements for approved executive workflows without assuming ownership of the Executive Workspace platform.

---

## Alignment Areas

AI Experience Engineering supports alignment around:

- Executive decision-support interactions.
- Contextual assistance.
- Recommendations.
- Conversational transitions.
- Workflow continuity.
- AI-to-workspace transitions.

---

# Executive Decision Support

The AI Experience should support approved decision-support interactions.

## Flow

EXECUTIVE CONTEXT
↓
AI INTERACTION
↓
APPROVED INSIGHT
↓
RECOMMENDATION
↓
CLARIFICATION
↓
DECISION SUPPORT

---

# Contextual Assistance

The experience may provide contextual AI interaction entry points where approved.

Relevant context must remain understandable to the user.

---

# Recommendations

Recommendations must:

- Remain distinguishable from user actions.
- Support clarification.
- Support follow-up.
- Preserve user control.

---

# Conversational Transitions

Where an AI interaction leads into an Executive Workspace workflow:

AI INTERACTION
↓
CONTEXTUAL TRANSITION
↓
EXECUTIVE WORKSPACE

The transition requirements must define interaction continuity without redefining Executive Workspace architecture.

---

# Workflow Continuity

The AI Experience should preserve relevant context across approved workflow transitions.

Where continuity cannot be safely inferred, clarification should be requested.

---

## Architectural Boundary

AI Experience Engineering:

- Defines AI interaction behavior.
- Defines interaction transitions.
- Supports workflow continuity.

AI Experience Engineering does not:

- Own the Executive Workspace platform.
- Redefine Executive Workspace architecture.
- Replace organizational reasoning authority.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

Executive Workspace alignment is complete when:

- Decision-support requirements are defined.
- Contextual assistance is defined.
- Recommendation behavior is defined.
- Conversational transitions are defined.
- Workflow continuity is defined.
- AI-to-workspace transitions are identified.
