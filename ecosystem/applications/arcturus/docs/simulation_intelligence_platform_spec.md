# Week 2 Objective

Define the constitutional architecture of the **Simulation Intelligence Platform** and specify how **validated simulation evidence becomes Organizational Intelligence** for the Organizational Brain (OBA).

## In Scope

- Simulation Intelligence Architecture
- Organizational Knowledge Extraction
- Pattern Discovery
- Prediction Framework
- Organizational Assessment
- Organizational Reasoning
- Recommendation Framework
- Confidence Levels and Ranking
- Explainability and Evidence Traceability
- Intelligence Lifecycle
- Knowledge Publication to OBA
- Cross-platform responsibilities

## Out of Scope

Week 2 does **not** include building predictive AI models, recommendation engines, or selecting a specific AI/ML framework, database, or implementation technology.

# Day 1 — Simulation Intelligence Architecture

## Architecture Flow

```text
Validated Simulation Evidence
        |
        v
Evidence Mapping
        |
        v
Knowledge Extraction
        |
        v
Pattern Discovery
        |
        v
Organizational Insights
        |
        v
Organizational Reasoning
        |
        v
Prediction / Assessment
        |
        v
Recommendations
        |
        v
Explainability + Evidence Traceability
        |
        v
Knowledge Publication
        |
        v
Organizational Brain (OBA)
```

**Boundary rule:** The Simulation Intelligence Platform does not execute simulations or validate experiments. It consumes only evidence that has already passed the Validation & Evaluation Platform.

## Required Intelligence Components

| Component | Specification |
|---|---|
| Intelligence Pipeline | Controls the movement of validated evidence into publishable intelligence. |
| Knowledge Extraction | Converts validated results into reusable organizational knowledge. |
| Pattern Discovery | Identifies recurring behaviors, trends, interactions, anomalies, and exceptions. |
| Organizational Insights | Converts discovered patterns into organizational meaning. |
| Intelligence Repository | Stores versioned intelligence artifacts and their evidence links. |
| Evidence Mapping | Connects every intelligence artifact to its source experiment and validation record. |
| Organizational Learning | Updates knowledge when new validated evidence supports, changes, or contradicts previous findings. |
| Explainability Layer | Explains what was concluded, why, from which evidence, and with what limitations. |
| Intelligence Lifecycle | Defines creation, review, publication, update, and deprecation. |
| Knowledge Publication | Publishes approved intelligence to OBA with evidence, confidence, explanation, and version information. |

## Knowledge Extraction Rule

A reusable knowledge record should contain:

- finding;
- organizational context;
- supporting validated evidence;
- assumptions or conditions;
- confidence level;
- source experiment and validation references;
- version.

The platform must distinguish **observation, association, prediction, and causal claim** so correlation is not presented as proven causation.

# Day 2 — Prediction, Assessment and Organizational Reasoning

## Reasoning Flow

```text
Validated Evidence
   -> Knowledge
   -> Pattern / Trend
   -> Organizational Insight
   -> Prediction / Assessment
   -> Risk / Opportunity / Root-Cause Analysis
   -> Recommendation
```

## Required Reasoning Concepts

| Concept | Specification |
|---|---|
| Prediction | Estimates a future organizational outcome under stated conditions. |
| Organizational Assessment | Evaluates the current or simulated condition of an organization or capability. |
| Root Cause Analysis | Identifies plausible causes while separating causal evidence from correlation. |
| Trend Detection | Identifies persistent change across simulations, scenarios, or time. |
| Risk Assessment | Evaluates possible negative outcomes, impact, conditions, and uncertainty. |
| Opportunity Identification | Identifies evidence-supported beneficial actions or conditions. |
| Organizational Health | Assesses overall organizational stability and effectiveness. |
| Capability Assessment | Evaluates whether required capabilities are present and effective. |
| Workforce Performance | Assesses workload, capacity, effectiveness, and workforce behavior. |
| Operational Efficiency | Evaluates workflow performance, time, capacity, and resource use. |
| Strategic Readiness | Assesses preparedness for a proposed change or future condition. |
| Confidence Levels | Communicates how strongly the evidence supports a conclusion. |

## Prediction Requirements

Every prediction should state:

- target outcome;
- organizational scope;
- scenario or conditions;
- prediction horizon;
- expected outcome;
- supporting validated evidence;
- confidence or uncertainty;
- assumptions and limitations.

If evidence is insufficient, the result should be **Insufficient Evidence** rather than an unsupported prediction.

## Confidence Levels

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| High | Strong, repeated, and consistent validated evidence. |
| Moderate | Useful evidence with meaningful limitations or uncertainty. |
| Low | Limited, weak, or conflicting evidence. |
| Insufficient | Evidence does not responsibly support the conclusion. |

# Day 3 — Recommendation Framework and Knowledge Integration

## Recommendation Flow

```text
Insight / Prediction / Assessment
        |
        v
Recommendation Candidate
        |
        v
Impact + Risk + Feasibility + Confidence Review
        |
        v
Priority / Ranking
        |
        v
Explanation + Evidence Trace
        |
        v
Review
        |
        v
Publication to OBA
```

## Required Recommendation Components

| Component | Specification |
|---|---|
| Recommendation Engine | Defines how evidence-based recommendation candidates are formed. |
| Action Prioritization | Orders candidate actions by organizational importance. |
| Decision Support | Presents alternatives, evidence, risks, and trade-offs for human decision-making. |
| Strategic Guidance | Supports long-term organizational decisions. |
| Operational Recommendations | Supports workflow, process, resource, or operational improvements. |
| Workforce Recommendations | Supports staffing, skills, workload, or workforce improvements. |
| Governance Recommendations | Supports policy, control, and governance improvements. |
| Confidence | Communicates evidence strength and uncertainty. |
| Recommendation Ranking | Orders options using visible decision criteria. |
| Explainability | Shows why a recommendation was produced. |
| Evidence Traceability | Links the recommendation to validated evidence. |
| Knowledge Integration | Stores and publishes recommendation knowledge for reuse by OBA. |

## Recommendation Record

Every recommendation should include:

- recommended action;
- objective;
- supporting evidence;
- expected benefit;
- risks and trade-offs;
- prerequisites;
- confidence;
- priority or rank;
- explanation;
- evidence trace.

## Recommendation Ranking

Ranking may consider:

1. evidence strength;
2. expected impact;
3. risk;
4. urgency;
5. feasibility;
6. strategic alignment;
7. confidence or uncertainty.

Exact numerical weights are **not fixed in Week 2** because they depend on organizational priorities and governance decisions.

## Explainability and Traceability

Every publishable intelligence artifact should answer:

1. What was concluded?
2. Why was it concluded?
3. Which validated evidence supports it?
4. What assumptions were made?
5. What is the confidence level?
6. What limitations or conflicting evidence exist?

Minimum traceability chain:

```text
Recommendation
 -> Prediction / Assessment / Insight
 -> Pattern / Knowledge Record
 -> Validated Simulation Evidence
 -> Validation Record
 -> Experiment / Scenario
```

# Day 4 — Review and Engineering Handover

## Cross-Platform Responsibilities

| Platform | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Validation & Evaluation Platform | Supplies scientifically validated evidence and validation metadata. |
| Simulation Intelligence Platform | Converts validated evidence into knowledge, predictions, assessments, and recommendations. |
| Organizational Brain (OBA) | Consumes approved intelligence for reasoning, memory, and decision-making. |
| Enterprise Ontology / Digital Twins | Provide shared organizational definitions, identities, relationships, and state context. |

**Rule:** Simulation Intelligence must never convert failed or unvalidated experimental results into organizational intelligence.

## Intelligence Lifecycle

```text
Validated Evidence
 -> Knowledge Extraction
 -> Pattern / Insight
 -> Reasoning
 -> Prediction / Assessment
 -> Recommendation
 -> Explainability + Review
 -> Publication to OBA
 -> New Validated Evidence
 -> Reinforce / Update / Deprecate
```

Previous versions must remain traceable when intelligence is updated or deprecated.

## OBA Publication Package

A published intelligence artifact should contain:

```text
Result
+ Organizational Context
+ Evidence References
+ Confidence
+ Explanation
+ Version
+ Review Status
```
